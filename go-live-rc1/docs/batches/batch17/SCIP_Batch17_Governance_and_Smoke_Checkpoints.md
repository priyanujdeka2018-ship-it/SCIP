# SCIP Batch 17 — Governance and Smoke Checkpoints

## Required roadmap item fields
Every roadmap item must include:
- strategic objective
- evidence
- owner
- success metric
- rollout gate
- risk
- dependency
- rollback / exit criteria
- Liquid Glass guardrail

## Smoke gates
- No Entity Head role appears.
- No third Arrival door appears.
- Roadmap is marked as L5 governance output.
- All roadmap items have evidence, owner, gate, success metric, and rollback criteria.
- Every quarter has an executive question and decision set.
- KPI/OKR tree includes objectives and measurable KRs.
- Benefits tracking has owner and review frequency.
- `roadmap.py` compiles.
- `main.py` includes the roadmap router.
- No frontend business computation is introduced.
