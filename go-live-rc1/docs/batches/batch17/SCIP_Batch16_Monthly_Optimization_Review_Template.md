# SCIP Batch 16 Monthly Optimization Review Template

## Meeting purpose
Turn post-launch adoption analytics into governed improvements without violating the Liquid Glass two-door experience.

## Cadence
Monthly, with emergency review only for trust, source freshness, RBAC, audit, or no-silent-fallback issues.

## Required attendees
- Platform owner
- Data owner
- Source owners for critical R-series reports
- Quickball owner
- UX/product owner
- MIS/QCG/Admin governance representative
- Finance representative when metric definitions or PR/TAT workflows are in scope
- CCO/GM/AGM representative when action queues/workflows are in scope

## Required evidence pack
1. Adoption summary: world/focus/depth usage, evidence opens, Quickball usage, action conversion.
2. Trust summary: stale sources, missing lineage, blocked Quickball answers, fallback disclosures.
3. Operational summary: action queues, workflow closure, notification effectiveness.
4. Source-owner SLA summary: missing/stale/late reports and corrective actions.
5. Defect trend: UAT-to-production defects and release regressions.

## Improvement item gate
Every item must include:
- Evidence refs
- Owner
- Decision state
- Expected impact
- Rollout gate
- Rollback criteria
- Liquid Glass guardrail
- Score and priority

## Decisions
Record each decision as approved, rejected, deferred, released, or rolled back.
