# SCIP Batch 17 — Quarterly Executive Operating Rhythm

## Operating principle
The rhythm converts platform evidence into decisions without creating a dashboard-first product. Executive cadence uses SCIP outputs as pre-read and evidence, while the application remains two-door: Live Pulse and Narratives.

## Weekly management rhythm
- **Owner:** CCO/GM/AGM
- **Inputs:** Live Pulse Risk & Action, action queue conversion, workflow stale/overdue status, notification suppression, forecast assumptions.
- **Output:** intervention decisions, owner follow-up, escalation closure, weekly exception list.
- **Guardrail:** no new weekly dashboard at Arrival; outputs are L5 action/evidence packs.

## Monthly optimization review
- **Owner:** Product Owner + MIS/QCG/Admin
- **Inputs:** Batch 15 adoption analytics, Batch 16 continuous-improvement backlog, source-owner SLA review, Quickball answer review, UX simplification review.
- **Output:** approve / hold / rollback / retire improvement items.
- **Gate:** every item must have evidence, owner, decision, expected impact, rollout gate, and rollback criteria.

## Quarterly executive steering committee
- **Owner:** Board/CXO sponsor
- **Inputs:** roadmap progress, benefits realization, major risks, roadmap decisions, DW/integration readiness, executive acceptance criteria.
- **Output:** strategic prioritization, quarterly release train approval, risk decisions, funding/resource direction.

## Annual planning cycle
- Month 10: refresh 12-month roadmap.
- Month 11: confirm enterprise integration and source-system cutover strategy.
- Month 12: executive benefits review and next-year OKR approval.
