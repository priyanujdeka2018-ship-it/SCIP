# SCIP Batch 15 Sample Adoption Dashboards

Adoption dashboards are not product entry points. They are governance/evidence briefs surfaced from Live Pulse → Risk & Action or MIS/QCG/Admin review.

## 1. Adoption Experience Brief

Purpose: understand whether users are following the intended Liquid Glass flow.

Cards:

- Live Pulse vs Narratives share.
- Focus distribution.
- Summary/Detailed/Executive depth usage.
- Quickball explanation usage.
- Evidence-path opens.
- Forecast review frequency.

Decision this brief supports: simplify one screen, strengthen one curiosity path, or improve one Quickball suggestion.

## 2. Operational Conversion Brief

Purpose: understand whether the platform is causing action.

Cards:

- Action queue open to conversion rate.
- Workflow closure rate.
- Stale workflow rate.
- Notification effectiveness.
- Role-level engagement.

Decision this brief supports: tune queue ranking, workflow drawer copy, or notification suppression.

## 3. Rollout Quality Brief

Purpose: understand production quality and trust risk.

Cards:

- Stale-source impact.
- UAT defect trend.
- Production defect escape rate.
- Quickball blocked-answer reasons.
- Missing lineage impact.

Decision this brief supports: prioritize source refresh, lineage repair, and UAT regression strengthening.
